Built to make paperwork simpler for families who live between languages.

Eazy Way Solutions started with a simple observation: many families lose time, money and peace of mind because important documents are prepared in a language they don't fully understand.

Today we prepare and translate documents for clients across the United States, with service in English, Portuguese and Spanish. We keep things organized, explain what each document is for and make sure you know exactly where it goes next.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    A short call or message in your language. We review your case and send a flat quote.

  2. 02

    We prepare everything

    Forms and translations are completed, then reviewed page by page with you before anything is signed.

  3. 03

    You file with confidence

    You receive the final set with clear instructions on where and how to file or submit it.

What a document preparer does, and what we don't do.

We prepare, format and translate documents based on the information and choices you give us. We are not attorneys, and when a situation calls for legal advice we will tell you so you can look for a lawyer.

  • We prepare and translate your documents
  • We explain the process and the filing steps
  • We do not give legal advice or represent you in court
  • We refer you to an attorney when your case needs one
