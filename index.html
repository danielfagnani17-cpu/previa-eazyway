Ask a Question
Are you a law firm?+

No. We are a document preparation service. We prepare and translate documents based on the information you provide, and we do not give legal advice. If your case needs an attorney, we will tell you.

Do I need to come to an office?+

Most services are done fully online or by phone. Interpreters meet you in person in Georgia, Alabama and Colorado.

Will my translation be accepted?+

Our certified translations include a signed statement of accuracy, the format required by USCIS, courts and most agencies.

How much does it cost?+

Every service has a flat price. You receive the quote after a free review, before you pay anything.

How long does it take?+

It depends on the service. Most translations are ready in a few business days, and we give you an estimated timeline together with your quote.
