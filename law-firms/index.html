Case Portfolio Review & Workflow Support

A clearer overview of your caseload. A more organized way to prioritize work.

As an immigration practice grows, keeping track of every open case, completed task, missing document and outstanding action can become increasingly challenging.

We can help your firm organize its existing case information into a structured Excel tracking document, with a practical overview of the cases and tasks identified through the records and information supplied by your team.

Caseload overview
Organize the cases included in the firm's review into a centralized tracking spreadsheet.
Work completed and outstanding
Record which tasks are documented as completed, which remain outstanding and which require clarification, based on the information provided.
Pending documentation
Identify missing documents or follow-up items already noted in the firm's records.
Priority identification
Help the team highlight cases or tasks that may need attention, based on the deadlines and priorities provided by the firm.
Action tracking
Create a clear list of outstanding tasks to help the team plan next steps.
Additional support opportunities
Identify areas where we may be able to assist with document preparation, organization or other agreed-upon project work.

A support tool, not a performance audit

Our goal is to give your firm a useful organizational resource, not to evaluate your staff or criticize existing workflows.

The review is collaborative and based on the case information, records and task updates provided by your firm. We do not independently access online case systems or verify case status with USCIS or other agencies as part of this service.

The resulting spreadsheet is meant to help your team understand the information available, identify outstanding items and decide where additional support may be useful. Legal decisions, case-status verification and final prioritization remain with your firm.

The goal is simple: greater visibility into your caseload, fewer overlooked tasks and additional support when your team needs it.

Flexible support for your firm

Not every firm needs the same kind of help. Some need support with one complicated filing. Others need help organizing a backlog, preparing document packages or keeping a clearer overview of their caseload.

We offer project-based support built around your firm's needs, workload and priorities, without a full-time staffing commitment. Whether it is one case, a complex RFE, a large evidence package, a case portfolio review or recurring document work, we agree on a scope that works for your team.
